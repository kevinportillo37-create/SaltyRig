Essential Knots
Words to know
Tag end
The short, loose end of the line you tie the knot with. Orange in the pictures.
Standing line
The part of the line that runs back to your rod. Blue in the pictures.
Eye
The hole in a hook, swivel, or lure that the line goes through.
Wrap
One full turn of the tag end around a line.
Snug
Pulled tight enough to hold its shape, but not cinched all the way.
